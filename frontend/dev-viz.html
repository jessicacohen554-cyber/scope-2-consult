Visualization Exploration

P35 — presentation alternatives tried against the real exports in data/. Linked from nowhere, in no nav; the deliverable is plans/mbm-dashboard/notes/P35-viz-recommendations.md.
What this page is. A throwaway comparison harness. Each question from prompts/P35-stub-viz-exploration.md gets a row of alternatives rendered from the same real data, so the choice is made on evidence rather than taste. Every prototype obeys DESIGN_SYSTEM.md (tokens only, no hex, no font literals) because the winners are meant to graduate into production. It loads the real exports by default; ?fixtures=1 switches to the hand-authored fixtures.
