On the burden and cost rows, 3 means “same as today” — not “neutral”. Those rows ask how internal effort, external service cost and EAC procurement prices would change relative to the respondent’s current practice, so a 4–5 there is a cost complaint, not opposition on the merits — which is why they carry the amber ramp and never the stance ramp.
