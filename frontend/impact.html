How this was measured
  • Mentions: keyword search of all free-text answers for four families: impact ("emissions / climate / carbon / decarbonization / real-world / grid impact", "impact metric / accounting / method / -based"), causal (causal, causality, causation, "would not otherwise"), consequential (consequential, avoided emissions, marginal emissions, emissionality) and additionality (additionality, incrementality). Bare "impact" does not count: the survey's own "integrity, impact and feasibility" wording is echoed in hundreds of answers.
  • Stance: each matching respondent's passages (±350 characters, impact-metric answers Q148/Q149/Q151 first, capped at 7,000 characters) were read against a fixed codebook: support, oppose, mixed or neutral on measuring, recognising or rewarding causal emissions impact. Using "climate impact" only as a yardstick for a proposal is neutral. Opposing hourly matching is not treated as opposing impact.
  • Where and which: supporters were also coded on venue (inside scope 2, separate metric, both, not said) and form (marginal / emissionality, additionality, avoided emissions, general); opponents on their main reason.
  • Q146–Q150: the survey's closed questions on a separate impact metric. Q147 and Q150 reasons are counted only for respondents whose Q146 answer opened that follow-up.
  • Checks: coding by AI coders; every oppose code and a random sample of support codes checked by hand; stances compared with the separate additionality coding where respondents overlap (—). No second-coder agreement score.
  • Names: as entered in the consultation, cleaned where the field held a job title or description. Redacted and individual filers are counted, never named.
  • Files: data/derived/impact_stance.csv, reference/impact_codebook.md, scripts/analytics/impact.py.
