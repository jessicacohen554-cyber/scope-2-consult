Additionality

Consultation section 7 · questions 26–34 — which tests should gate a consequential claim, and which could actually be run
What this page covers. Additionality is the question of whether an intervention changed anything that would not have happened anyway. The consultation put nine candidate tests to respondents, asked how demanding each should be, then asked separately which of them are implementable at all — and whether the answer should change by region or by the strength of the claim being made. Every figure below is read from the exported dataset. Percentages always carry the base they divide by, and segment cells with fewer than five respondents are suppressed rather than printed.
