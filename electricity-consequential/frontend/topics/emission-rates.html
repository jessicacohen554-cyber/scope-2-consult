Marginal emission rates

Consultation section 8 · questions 35–46 — operating margin, build margin, and how fine the resolution should go
What this page covers. If a consequential method is going to say what an intervention changed, it needs a marginal emission rate to measure the change against. This section of the consultation asked which methodologies produce a defensible one — separately for the operating margin and the build margin — and how finely such a rate should be resolved in space and time.
