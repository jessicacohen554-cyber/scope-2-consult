Read this page first. A consultation is not a poll and, at this size, not a survey either: respondents self-select, a handful of coordinated filings move a count visibly, and the room empties as the questionnaire goes on. Before any stance elsewhere on this site means anything, it matters who filed, how much of the form they finished, and whether they attached a name to it — because none of the three are evenly distributed.

Which way each question moves

An attributable-only reading is a partial reading. Quote only the filings you can cite by name, build the story from the organizations willing to be identified, and the panel above is the size of what you are leaving out. That is a caution about method, not about motive: withholding a name is a disclosure choice available to every respondent, this project never names a redacted filer, and nothing in the split tells you anything about any individual.

How to read this page

Organization is uncontrolled free text. The form asked for an organization name and accepted whatever was typed: real names, placeholders, job titles, and in at least one case a paragraph of prose describing the filer. Names in the browser are truncated for display and are otherwise shown exactly as submitted, so some rows read like sentences. Nothing joins on the name — every lookup keys on the respondent id — and a name that looks like a placeholder has not been corrected or guessed at.

Redacted is not illegitimate. Those respondents answered the same questions and their answers carry the same weight in every count on this site; they simply cannot be attributed, audited, or quoted by name, and this site never names them. The honest reading is that attributable and withheld responses are two populations with measurably different stances, and the withheld one is large.

Self-declared fields are self-declared. Organization type, sector, country and the inventory questions are the respondent's own answers, not verified attributes. The audited class column in the browser is this project's reading of what a named organization actually is, with the basis stated inside each profile; where the two disagree, both are shown. Redacted respondents have no audited class, because there is nothing to check.

Small segments are suppressed, not zero. Any segment with fewer than five respondents arrives from the exporter as a suppression sentinel. Panels on this page leave those rows out of the chart and say how many they are leaving out; they are never drawn as zero and never printed.

Free text was capped at 4,000 characters. Answers that land exactly on the cap are marked likely truncated where they appear in a profile — the submitted text continued past what was stored.

This page is the analytical base only. Excluded filings appear nowhere on this page — not in a chart, not in the browser, not in a profile. They are documented, with the evidence and the rule that caught them, on the integrity & evidence page.
