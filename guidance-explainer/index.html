The map

Four questions, one framework

Everything on this site sits somewhere on the Actions and Market Instruments working group's draft multi-statement framework: a physical inventory, a market-based inventory, and a separate impact statement. The pages trace each topic to its statement, and say when no document has yet placed it.

How to read these pages

Three tiers, two labels

In force

GHG Protocol standards and guidance a company can be held to today: the Corporate Standard, the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance, the Project Protocol and Grid Guidelines, the Land Sector and Removals Standard v1.1 and Guidance v1.0.

Revision

Material from the standards development and governance repository: working-group decks and minutes, board minutes, consultation drafts, requests for information, feedback summaries, and GHG Protocol blog posts. Direction, not rules.

Third party

Registries, certificate schemes, regulations such as the US 45V rule and EU delegated acts, technical baselines such as NETL, peer-reviewed work, and consultation respondents. Used only where GHG Protocol material is silent.

Inference

Reasoning built from quoted text. Each inference names the passages it rests on. Treat it as a position to test, not a finding.

Gap

A question no retrieved document answers. Gaps are where advocacy language is still open and where a stakeholder can shape the outcome.
