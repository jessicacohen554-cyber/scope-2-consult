1 · The update in one screen

Two market-based methods, one destination

The ISB has pointed the revision at a stricter "Method 1" and would keep the 2015 rules alive as "Method 2" until a year not yet chosen.

On 16 October 2026 GHG Protocol published "Scope 2 Market-based Method Update: From 2025-2026 Public Consultation to Current Status". It reports what the Independent Standards Board (ISB) has asked for since the public consultation closed, and what it has left to the Scope 2 Technical Working Group (TWG). GHGP blog, MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026

The Secretariat proposed "a role for two market-based methods that correspond with the two theories of change that emerged from the public consultation feedback". The working names are Method 1 and Method 2, and the names themselves are still open. MBM Update PDF, p.2 S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 40

Method 1
“This approach would incorporate hourly matching, deliverability and incrementality accounting and reporting requirements.”
MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026 · MBM Update PDF, p.2
Method 2
“This approach would maintain existing scope 2 requirements from the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance, allowing organizations to continue their current procurement strategies and addressing stakeholder concerns regarding feasibility and market participation.”
MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026 · MBM Update PDF, p.2

The Secretariat gave the ISB three options for its September 2026 meeting: two methods with different metrics, two tCO2e methods "in perpetuity", or two tCO2e methods "with a phased transition from method 2 to method 1 over time". GHGP blog, MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026 The deck records the in-perpetuity option as the "Least preferred option by ISB members" (individual members' views), and the resulting hybrid as "deemed to further improve alignment with the decision-making criteria". S2 TWG #23 deck, slides 36, 38

The ISB then proposed this direction of travel, "to be further refined by the Scope 2 TWG":

“Companies [shall/should/may, language still to be determined] report under the market-based method if data that meets the quality criteria is available. If a company does report scope 2 market-based method emissions:

- Companies may report using Method 2 (current market-based method under 2015 Scope 2 Guidance) until a to-be-determined date,

- Companies should report using Method 1 until a to-be-determined date,

- Prior to that date, companies shall disclose whether they are applying market-based Method 1 or Method 2, and

- After that date, if a company reports scope 2 market-based method emissions, companies shall report using Method 1 if data that meets the quality criteria is available.”

MBM Update, "Updated Direction of Travel" · GHGP blog, 16 Oct 2026
Fuller deck version: adds "203X", "Statement 2" and the LBM "shall" (slide 39)

The source text is cut off after "quality"; the bracketed word is this site's. · S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 39
Signal of direction · September 2026 ISB

The ISB "agreed that Method 1 is what Scope 2 should transition to, with the pathway being the open question, and recorded a pulse check in which 10 of 10 ISB members indicated support. The Secretariat emphasized that a pulse check gives direction and is not a decision." S2 TWG #23 minutes, 6 Oct 2026, p.5

Two things stay open by design. First, whether reporting the market-based method is required at all: the Secretariat told the TWG that "whether the market-based method is required at all is itself an open question for the TWG". Second, when the method applies. A company reporting market-based emissions "would be required to use the new MBM, maintaining continuity with the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance under which the market-based method is not required in markets where qualifying data is unavailable." S2 TWG #23 minutes, pp.5–6

The ISB's stated rationale, as relayed by the Secretariat, rests on credibility: "Lack of a physical connection in the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance (e.g., Method 2) is a critical and visible credibility issue for the GHG Protocol." S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 37

Gap

No source sets the value of 203X, the age limit "[X] yrs" for incrementality, or the deliverability boundaries. Option C in the September materials used a "Transition/Legacy period (until [~2033-2035])", and ISB members' comments on Option A "Anticipate significant data improvements by 2030". Neither is a decision. S2 TWG #23 deck, slides 35, 38, 40

2 · From draft to direction

What changed between the 2025 consultation draft and September 2026

The September direction adds a third pillar, places size thresholds outside Method 1, proposes no legacy clause beyond the transition timeline, and leaves standard supply service open. ISO's working draft differs on time matching, deliverability, legacy contracts and standard supply service.

The October 2025 consultation proposed two tests for every certificate in the market-based method: hourly matching and deliverability. The ISB-approved proposal would require that organizations only report energy attribute certificates if they are "matched to hourly electricity consumption (hourly matching), and" "connected to deliverable grid regions where the organization consumes electricity (deliverability)." GHGP blog, MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026 The table sets that draft against Method 1 as presented for the September 2026 ISB meeting, and against ISO Working Group 4's second working draft (WD2) of ISO 14064-1 as presented in the same TWG deck.

The TWG raised the ISO gap directly. Members "raised concerns that the ISO direction differs materially from the ISB direction"; the Secretariat replied that "ISO feedback comes to the TWG to consider before recommendations go to the ISB." S2 TWG #23 minutes, pp.6–7

Inference

The ISO divergence is the largest external risk to the ISB direction. The joint standard needs ISO approval, which requires "a two-thirds majority of permanent members of ISO/TC 207/SC 7" in favour and "no more than one-quarter of the total number of votes cast" negative. This site draws this inference; no GHG Protocol source states it. Consolidated SDP, 29 Jul 2026, p.16

3 · The numbers

The consultation numbers, set against the direction

Most respondents rated hourly matching and deliverability low. Nine in ten supported a legacy clause. Additionality was never put to a rated question.

The Scope 2 public consultation ran from 20 October 2025 to 31 January 2026 and drew "nearly 1100" responses from 56 countries. Companies, industry groups and consultants were 62% of respondents; energy suppliers, retailers and utilities were 8.5%. S2 consultation exec summary, p.2 Each question below was rated on a scale of 1 to 5. The summary groups 4–5 as support, 3 as neutral and 1–2 as no or low support. The Secretariat's position on how the numbers count is on record. Responding to TWG members on 6 October 2026, it said "the consultation is not a voting exercise and that feedback is assessed against the decision-making criteria". S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.6

4 · Incrementality

How a no-further-restrictions position became an age limit

Between March 2025 and September 2026, incrementality went from rejected by the TWG, to parked with the AMI TWG, to part of Method 1.

STEP 1 · MARCH 2025

The TWG and the ISB both said no

The TWG was asked: "Should voluntary procurement be required to meet additional criteria that restricts eligible supply of CFE? Yes: 0 / Mixed: 3 / No: 31 / Needs more information: 3". Only 3 chose incrementality criteria; 25 chose none of the listed restrictions. S2 TWG #10 minutes, 19 Mar 2025, p.4

Eight days later an ISB pulse check showed broad support for "Defining voluntary procurement eligibility based on time matching, deliverability, and fair allocation of SSS, without introducing further restrictions under the Scope 2 MBM." ISB #8 summary, 27 Mar 2025, p.4

STEP 2 · JULY–OCTOBER 2025

Parked with the AMI TWG

In a formal vote (Yes 4, No 7), the ISB declined to send the consequential subgroup's marginal-emissions proposal to consultation and "directed this work to continue within the Actions and Market Instruments TWG". It also "encouraged the Scope 2 workstream to include questions about electric sector avoided emissions, additionality and related topics in the Public Consultation." ISB #12 summary, 14 Jul 2025, pp.2–3

In September the ISB Chair told the Scope 2 TWG "that these questions fall within the remit of the Actions and Market Instruments (AMI) TWG". S2 TWG #19 minutes, 17 Sep 2025, p.3 The consultation proposal rested on two pillars, hourly matching and deliverability, and did not ask a rated question on additionality. GHGP blog, MBM Update, 16 Oct 2026 S2 consultation summary v1.1, p.114

STEP 3 · JUNE–SEPTEMBER 2026

The AMI TWG puts impact into Statement 2

At its Amsterdam meeting the AMI TWG discussed three options for Statement 2. "Option 3 was agreed: Additionality/impact required for Statement 2." Details "for how to operationalize it" were left "TBD". AMI TWG #19 deck, 15 Jul 2026, slide 20

By September a majority of the subgroup "considered that there should be at least impact requirements for Statement 2… what is reported in Statement 2 should have a demonstrated impact going beyond business as usual." AMI TWG #20 minutes, 9 Sep 2026, p.6

STEP 4 · RESPONSE DATED 24 AUGUST 2026

A scientific-accuracy complaint

Authors from WattTime, DTU, Edinburgh and Sweco argued that the proposal "introduces requirements for deliverable and time-matched energy but excludes the principle of additionality", and that it was justified by studies of all three pillars. The complaint itself is undated. They "take no position on whether the GHG Protocol should adopt the Three Pillars". WattTime et al. complaint, p.2

The ISB Chair replied that the letter "was classified as a complaint" and that "the topics you raised will be given due consideration by the Scope 2 Technical Working Group and subject to further rigorous review by the Independent Standards Board." ISB Chair response, 24 Aug 2026, p.1

STEP 5 · SEPTEMBER 2026

Method 1 gets an age limit

Method 1 as presented for the September ISB meeting includes "Age limit on eligible resources for voluntary claims (shall)". S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 34 The Secretariat said "the ISB supported its inclusion on integrity and impact grounds, that it is open for development by the TWG". S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.6

The number of years is still "[X]".

STEP 6 · 6 OCTOBER 2026

The TWG pushes back

Members said incrementality "is relevant to assessing impact but not necessary for allocating emissions attributes to consumption, and that it should sit outside the inventory". They also "raised concerns that the direction departs from the TWG's earlier work and from the balance of consultation feedback". S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.6

Afterwards "the ISB Chair wrote to the TWG to ask that they answer the questions provided by the ISB". "Proposals for a different direction will be captured after the workshop for the ISB to consider alongside TWG recommendations." S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 42

Inference

The ISB's September move matches both the AMI TWG's July agreement and the complaint's argument. No source says that either one caused it. This site flags both links as inference only.

6 · Position

What to take into a position

Takeaway

As of 19 October 2026 the ISB has named an hourly, deliverable, incremental Method 1 as the destination. Everything that would bind a reporter is still open: the year 203X, the age limit, the boundaries, and whether the market-based method is required at all. The next scheduled ISB decision point is 15 December 2026.

  • Sourced. The direction is a 10-of-10 pulse check, and "a pulse check gives direction and is not a decision". Before 203X, Method 2 "may", Method 1 "should" and disclosure "shall". S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.5 GHGP blog, 16 Oct 2026
  • Sourced. TWG members said the direction "departs from the TWG's earlier work and from the balance of consultation feedback" (70% no or low support for hourly matching; 90% support for a legacy clause), and the Secretariat says "the consultation is not a voting exercise". S2 consultation summary v1.1 S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.6
  • Sourced. ISO WG4's draft keeps monthly matching as the minimum, makes hourly optional, and removes deliverability and SSS. Reconciliation runs through the TWG and then the ISB. S2 TWG #23 minutes, pp.6–7
  • Inference. Statement 3 electricity methods are slated to start only in Q2 2027 (an ISB pulse check; resourcing rests with the Steering Committee and CEO), so the route the ISB names for non-Method-1 procurement will probably not be fully specified in the Q2 2027 draft. S2 TWG #23 minutes, p.5
  • Gap. The value of 203X, the "[X] yrs" age limit, the deliverability boundaries and the September ISB minutes are not public.
  • Inference. Because these are open, the ISB's own questions to the TWG (earliest feasible date, regions with data gaps, evidence on market investment, SSS strength) are where new evidence meets an undecided point. S2 TWG #23 deck, slide 40
