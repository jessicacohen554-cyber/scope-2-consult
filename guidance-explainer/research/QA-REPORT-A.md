# QA report, shard A (ccs-removals, ccs-eac, scope2-update, governance)

- Date: 2026-10-19
- Scope: site/ccs-removals.html and site/ccs-eac.html against research/R1-lsrg-ccs-eac.md; site/scope2-update.html and site/governance.html against research/R2-scope2-update-governance.md. sources.html was read only.
- Method: every section read against the dossier. Every quoted span was checked by script against the dossier text: per-block pairing of straight and curly quotes, with ellipses splitting the quote into fragments. Every cite href and tier was checked against the row ids and tiers in sources.html. Render check: copy of shoot.js on port 8801 (qa-a/shoot-a.js). Figure check: qa-a/figs.js measures SVG text size at drawn scale, text outside the viewBox and text overlap, in both toggle states, at 400 and 1280 px, light and dark. Phone screenshots of each figure were inspected.
- Severity key. Blocker: false or mis-sourced fact. Fix: overstatement, mislabelled inference, wrong attribution, invented count, missing cite chip, or unreadable figure. Nit: wording or style.
- Result: 0 blockers, 36 fix, 18 nit. Of the 36 fix-level items, 33 were edited in the pages and 3 remain. Two are scrolly figures whose phone text cannot be enlarged without breaking the 40vh rule. The third is a tier mismatch in sources.html, which this shard may not edit.
