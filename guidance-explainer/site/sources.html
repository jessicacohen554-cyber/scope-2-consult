How to read a chip

Tiering is the method

Pages lean on in-force GHG Protocol text first, then on revision materials from the governance repository, then on third parties. Inference and gap callouts mark where no source speaks.

A chip such as Scope 2 Guidance 2015 §7.1 means the sentence before it is supported by that document at that place. Where a dossier recorded that a document could not be retrieved, the row says so in its title.
